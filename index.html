What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO earns placement in the classic list of search results. AEO earns placement inside an answer box or AI Overview. GEO earns a named citation inside a generative AI recommendation. Well-structured, genuinely useful content helps all three at once.

Why does programmatic SEO work?

Search demand is long-tail: for every broad head term there are hundreds of specific, lower-volume queries — one city plus one service, one product plus one use case. Writing a page for each of those by hand does not scale, but a template filled with genuine, row-specific data does. The discipline is to keep every page meaningfully different: unique facts, unique examples, unique answers. Volume without substance is what gives programmatic SEO a bad name; substance at volume is what makes it work.

How does 3x Visibility help with local SEO?

Local businesses need one landing page per location, and each page needs the details that make it real: the address, the services offered there, the neighbourhoods covered and the answers customers in that area ask. 3x Visibility imports that location data, maps it into a template built from your own design, and generates a complete, internally linked set of local pages — each scored for SEO quality before it ships.

How does 3x Visibility handle structured data?

Every generated page can include JSON-LD structured data automatically. The platform supports LocalBusiness, FAQPage, Product, Service, BreadcrumbList and other schema types depending on the template configuration. This means each page you publish is immediately machine-readable by search engines and AI systems without manual markup work.

What is internal linking and why does it matter?

Internal linking connects your generated pages to each other and to your existing site content. When a location page links to a related service page, and a service page links back to the category hub, search engines understand the topical relationship between pages. 3x Visibility can automatically insert contextual internal links during generation based on rules you define in your template, strengthening the site architecture without manual effort.

How do businesses create pages at scale?

The process follows four steps: first, prepare a structured data source such as a CSV file containing one row per page you need. Second, build a template that defines the page structure, variables and content blocks. Third, map your data columns to template variables so each row fills the template with its own unique information. Fourth, generate and publish the entire batch in one operation. Each page receives its own SEO score before publishing, so you can filter out low-quality outputs before they go live.

What is AEO and how does 3x Visibility support it?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization — the practice of structuring content so that AI-powered answer engines (Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot, Perplexity, ChatGPT Search) can extract and cite your content directly in their responses. 3x Visibility supports AEO by generating pages with clear question-based headings, concise direct answers in the first sentence of each section, and FAQPage structured data that marks up Q&A pairs in a machine-readable format. Because generated pages are published as static HTML, their full content is accessible to crawlers and AI retrieval systems without requiring JavaScript execution.

What is GEO and why does it matter?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization — getting your brand cited by name inside generative AI recommendations. When someone asks an AI assistant "what tool can I use to create hundreds of SEO pages?", GEO determines whether your product is mentioned in the answer. The signals that help are: clear entity definitions (what the product is, who it serves, what category it belongs to), consistent brand terminology across the web, structured data that identifies your product, and authoritative content that AI systems can verify and reference. 3x Visibility generates pages with all these signals built in — Organization schema, SoftwareApplication schema, consistent brand naming, and topical depth that establishes entity authority.

How is 3x Visibility different from manual page creation?

Creating pages manually means writing each one individually in a CMS editor — a process that takes 30 to 60 minutes per page when you include research, writing, formatting, SEO checks and publishing. For a campaign of 200 location pages, that is 100 to 200 hours of work. 3x Visibility reduces this to the time needed to prepare a data file and configure a template — typically under two hours total — then generates and publishes all 200 pages in a single batch. Each page is unique because it is filled with row-specific data: different locations, different services, different facts and different answers.
